- **Application Expenses (`A33:E42`)**: Tracks mandatory medical, psych, NBI, and school credentials totaling **`₱2,420.00`**.
- **Dentist & Braces Orthodontic Tracker (`A44:E67`)**:
  - Full billed items (`₱37,500.00`) and deductible payment installments (`-₱8,000.00`).
  - Supports both pure dentist remaining balance (**`₱29,500.00`**) and the combined `D67` debt balance (**`₱31,920.00`**).
  - Visual unicode progress bar (**`██░░░░░░░░`**, 21.33%) displayed on both the Apt Expenses view and the Executive Dashboard.
- **Group & Personal Ledgers**: S&R Food (`₱746.00`), Ate Joy & Lorraine mini-ledgers (`₱250.00`).
- **Noella & James Expedition (`A126:F135`)**: Dynamic N-person divisor formula `=IFERROR(ROUNDUP(Price*Count/Divisor, 0), 0)` supporting arbitrary group sizes (1, 3, or 5 people, Benchmark Subtotal **`₱968.00`**).

  - Full multi-party settlement matrix for Troy, Jaco, and Dan.
  - Automatic injection of Spotify share (`₱57.40`) and utilities share from `Monthly Bills!P6` (`₱3,175.00`).

### 6. Dynamic Annual Holiday Engine & Interactive Observance Manager
- **Elimination of Hardcoded Holiday Dates**:
  - Replaced static annual holiday arrays with an astronomical and algorithmic dynamic calendar engine in `app/core/calendar_engine.py`.
  - **Meeus/Jones/Butcher Anonymous Gregorian Computus**: Dynamically computes Easter Sunday, Maundy Thursday (200%), Good Friday (200%), and Black Saturday (130%) for any calendar year ($2020 \le \text{year} \le 2035+$).
  - **National Heroes Day (RA 9492)**: Dynamically computes the last Monday of August.
  - **Islamic & Lunar Observances**: Lookups for Eid'l Fitr, Eid'l Adha, and Chinese New Year across 2020–2035.
- **Interactive Holiday Manager Modal & User Controls**:
  - Year selector (`2024`–`2030`) dynamically refreshes all statutory dates.
  - **External Holiday Calendar API Integration**: Sync live Philippine holiday feeds via public Nager.Date API (`https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/{year}/PH`) with automatic legal pay classification (Regular 200%, Special Non-Working 130%, Special Working 100%) and graceful offline fallback.
  - **Holiday CRUD (Add / Remove / Toggle)**:
    - Dedicated `➕ Add Holiday` drawer for institutional, municipal, or university foundation days.
    - Explicit `🗑️ Remove` button on every holiday row for instantaneous deletion.
    - Interactive toggling of `is_active` status for each holiday with immediate reactive re-calculation of workdays, units, holiday losses, and net cash flow.
  - Inline date editing to adapt to Presidential Proclamations and holiday economics long-weekend shifts.
  - One-click statutory reset back to Philippine legal baselines.
