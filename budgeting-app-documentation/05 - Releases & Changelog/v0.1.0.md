# Release v0.1.0: Initial Release & Core Engine Parity

**Release Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Canonical Version Badge:** `Release v0.1.0`  
**Git Branch:** `dev`  
**Platform:** Windows 10/11 x64  

---

## 🚀 Overview

`Release v0.1.0` marks the initial working desktop release of the **Personal Budgeting & Philippine Salary/Tax Suite**. Built with Python, PyWebView, and an Emerald/Obsidian glassmorphic interface, this release achieves 100% mathematical parity against both the Pinasweldo Philippine statutory calculators and the Dan Budgeting Excel workbook (`references/Dan BudgetingV3.xlsx`).

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Implemented Modules

### 1. High-Precision Decimal Math Core
- **Zero Floating-Point Drift:** Powered strictly by `decimal.Decimal` with explicit Excel rounding (`ROUNDUP`, `ROUND_HALF_UP`).
- **Pinasweldo Parity:** Reverse-engineered 2026 SSS (Regular + MPF + EC step ladder up to ₱35k MSC), PhilHealth (5% floor ₱10k / ceiling ₱100k), Pag-IBIG (₱200 cap), and BIR TRAIN Law withholding tax tables.
- **13th-Month Pay Exemption:** Automatic segregation of bonuses into tax-exempt (₱90k threshold) and taxable excess.
- **Freelance & Self-Employed Mode:** Side-by-side comparison between 8% Gross Income Tax and Graduated Rates with 40% OSD.

### 2. Bi-Weekly Calendar & Workday Engine
- **Excel `NETWORKDAYS.INTL` Parity:** Native implementation with 7-day binary weekday masks (`0111111` for Monday-only, etc.) and automatic Philippine statutory holiday exclusions.
- **Cutoff Accumulator:** Accurate tracking of working days, teaching unit accumulation, and daily transport/food living budget across Cutoff 1 (1–15) and Cutoff 2 (16–EOM).

### 3. Multi-Tenant Utility Splitter & Peer Ledgers
- **Dynamic Multi-Occupant Division:** Implements `=Total / COUNTIF(tenants, TRUE)` with instant toggle for active occupants.
- **3-Month Rolling Utility Averages:** Tracks historical trends across Rent, Water, Electricity, and Internet.
- **Calumpang 50/50 Roundup Ledger:** Implements `=ROUNDUP(Price/2, 0)` for shared expense reconciliation.
- **Mama Groceries Remittance:** Implements `=SUM(Items) - SUM(Deductions)` net transfer calculations.

### 4. Executive Cash Flow Waterfall Dashboard
- Full cash flow waterfall from gross income down to net discretionary surplus.
- Real-time savings rate percentage gauge and financial health metrics.

### 5. Local Storage & Privacy Shield
- Strictly isolates personal financial data into local git-ignored user storage (`%APPDATA%/PersonalBudgetSuite` or `.user_data/`).
- Includes one-click **Import from Excel** and **Export Backup** utilities.
